- **Reference tower T4 — the ADS cable growth judges both sources on a genset-backed path (2026-11-13)**: `computeSystem`'s `grownSolve` runs its growth (`growPass`: T3 feeders, then the Round-56 finals, each with its own hopeless revert) as a UTILITY pass and then, on TN with a standby genset (`gensetFaultBasis` non-null), a BOTH-SOURCES pass. Bounds are as in the utility pass. A way the second pass cannot cure is restored to its UTILITY-pass floor, never below it, and `ads-disconnection-on-genset` stays. With no genset, TT, or no backed board, the result is the utility pass verbatim. `gensetAdsCheck` stays judge-only.
- **Reference tower T3 — an auto-sized feeder cable grows for its own ADS and its downstream cumulative drop (2026-11-13)**: in `computeSystem` (`grownSolve`, before the Round-56 final-way growth) a FEEDER whose cable is auto-sized (no `cableOverrideMm2`, one run per phase) steps up `standardSectionsMm2` one rung at a time, the whole `solveAll` pipeline re-run each time. It grows (a) on TN while the feeder itself fails automatic disconnection for its device (`adsCsaFloorMm2`, `cableRaisedForAds`, INFO `cable-raised-for-ads`); and (b) while an auto-sized final way of a board below it has `cumulativeDropPercent` > its limit with its OWN drop within the limit (`feederVdCsaFloorMm2`, `cableRaisedForDownstreamVd`). For (b) the growth goes to the nearest growable feeder up that way's chain. The bound is the ladder top. A feeder still failing at the top reverts to its load-sized cable and keeps its warning. A user-fixed cable never grows. A way whose own drop is over its limit is left to its own VD rule. The device selectivity floor follows the larger cable's Iz (Round 40 / E3 unchanged: the floor itself never inflates copper). Order-independent: feeders and panels are visited in sorted order and each iteration applies every wanted rung at once.
- **Reference tower T2 — a combined system's sprinkler feed main is enlarged before the sprinkler may set the pump head (2026-11-13)**: `enlargeCombinedSprinklerFeedMains` (`sizing/sprinkler_feed_main.dart`) runs on the FINAL sizing in `sizingDetailProvider`, after the jockey pass and the recirc-loop resize. - **Reference tower T1 — past a discharge header only a full-rated main is discharge pipe (2026-11-13)**: in `raiseFirePumpSuctionAndDischarge`, once the walk is past a discharge HEADER (a junction where pump legs merge), an outflow — a run onward, or a riser leaving the header-state node — is NFPA 20 discharge piping only when its OWN sized flow is at least the pump's rated flow (`carriesRated`); every other outflow keeps its own class size and flow.
